The reward structure of Predictive Victory Dynamics is built on two payout streams that run in parallel — small per-round payouts that keep your Victory Emblems growing, and a final performance conversion that pays out when the bracket ends. Understanding both, and the milestones worth aiming for, is the difference between exiting the event with a solid haul and discovering you left Primogems on the table. This guide itemizes the full reward structure and the claim process, including the deadlines that catch players out.

## The Two Reward Streams

**Per-round payouts** settle after every daily resolve. Each correct prediction returns your stake plus winnings, and the accumulating Emblem bank is what funds your later bets — early accuracy literally compounds, because an accurate first week gives you a bigger bankroll to press advantages with in the semifinal and final rounds where stakes and payouts are at their peak.

**Final conversion rewards** settle when the event closes. Your finishing Emblem total and your accuracy record convert into the headline prizes: Primogems as the flagship currency, plus Mora, Character Talent Materials, Weapon Ascension materials and Mystic Enhancement Ore scaled to your final standing. The conversion is tiered — cross each performance threshold and every category of the payout improves, which is why strategic players track their tier standing, not just their bankroll.

## The Reward Tiers

The final conversion follows a tiered structure. Participation tier is guaranteed for everyone who joined and played through the rounds — a modest baseline of Primogems and Mora that makes the event worth a few minutes a day for anyone. From there, each accuracy tier steps up every reward category: the mid tiers add meaningful material quantities, and the top tiers — reserved for players who called the bracket with sustained accuracy across all eight rounds — pay out the event's full value, including its complete Primogem ceiling and the largest material bundles.

Two structural notes matter for planning. First, the tiers read accuracy, not bankroll size alone — a player who bet small but predicted well can out-tier a player who bet big and predicted average, which the scoring guide decomposes in full. Second, the daily task rewards feed the same pipeline: they top up your Emblem bank every day of the event, and the days you skip are top-ups you permanently forgo.

## Milestone Targets Worth Aiming For

For players optimizing their outcome rather than just participating, three milestones organize the event:

**The full-participation milestone.** Place at least one prediction in every round and claim every daily task. This alone secures the participation tier and maximizes your bankroll for the late event, where per-match payouts are largest.

**The accuracy milestone.** Finish with a correct-call record above the community average. The in-event interface tracks your hit rate against the field — a record consistently above the crowd puts the mid reward tiers within reach even with conservative stakes.

**The bankroll milestone.** Convert a top-percentile Emblem total at close. This is where aggressive-but-disciplined players separate: the betting strategy guide covers how scaled staking on high-confidence boards builds a bankroll that the final conversion rewards disproportionately.

## What the Rewards Are Actually Worth

For banner planning: a top-tier Predictive Victory Dynamics performance pays out on the order of a meaningful fraction of a ten-pull in Primogems, plus enough Mora and talent materials to noticeably advance a character or two. No single Genshin Impact event funds a pity run, but as free daily-engagement events go, this one's ceiling is generous relative to its time cost — a few minutes a day for a payout that scales with skill rather than account power.

## How and When to Claim

Claims happen in the event panel, and the timing rules are strict:

1. **Per-round payouts** flow automatically on resolve — nothing to claim manually.
2. **Daily task rewards** must be claimed manually from the event task list, and expire when the next daily reset arrives. Unclaimed task rewards are the most commonly lost payout in the entire event.
3. **Final conversion rewards** appear after the grand final resolves and must be claimed before the event page closes. HoYoverse events do not mail these automatically — an unclaimed final payout is gone when the panel disappears. Set a reminder for the day after the final; this single habit saves more rewards than any prediction improvement.

## FAQ

**Do I lose unclaimed rewards if I stop playing?** Per-round payouts are safe, but daily task rewards expire at each daily reset and final conversion rewards expire when the event ends. The claim guide above is the short version: claim dailies daily, claim the final on day one of the grace window.

**Is it better to save Emblems for late rounds or spend early?** Late rounds pay more per correct call, but reaching them with a healthy bankroll requires surviving the early rounds. The strategy guide's answer: stake proportionally to confidence all event, and let the bankroll grow into the late boards naturally.

**How does my accuracy tier get calculated?** Correct calls across all placed predictions, weighted toward consistency — the exact scoring mechanics, including how streaks factor in, are broken down in the scoring and payout guide.

**Are the rewards the same on all servers?** The reward structure is consistent across servers for this event; regional differences, if any appear, will be noted here when confirmed.

The bracket decides who wins the event; the claim rules decide who keeps the winnings. Play the rounds, claim the dailies, and be there the day after the final.
