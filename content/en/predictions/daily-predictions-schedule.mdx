## How We Build Each Day's Picks

Every prediction published here follows the same five-step method, so you can audit any call against the same evidence we used:

1. **Roster audit** — damage sources versus enablers on both sides; a team's true output lives in its carries and its batteries, not its headline names.
2. **Energy economy check** — Energy Recharge distribution across each roster; burst frequency is the strongest single predictor of simulation outcomes.
3. **Reaction mapping** — which team owns the elemental reaction tempo and can sustain it through a long fight.
4. **Form line** — how each team looked in prior resolves: dominant wins, scraped wins, flattering losses. Late in the bracket, form outweighs paper stats.
5. **Crowd read** — the in-event prediction percentages. We track where the public money goes and why; the best value plays are usually the ones where the crowd has overcorrected to a name instead of a composition.
